**Viscenario – Constructions companies’ best friend**

Most construction companies have a large scope of routines, checklists, documents, inspections, safety rounds, issues / defects and follow-ups of subcontractors. Often there is no overall overview. Without control, thousands of dollars will disappear from the bottom line every single year.  
Viscenario offers a solution that gives your business the opportunity to get rid of pen and paper, as well as unnecessary expensive time spent with duplicate work in the project. The solution is as effective as it is simple and user-friendly – and information security is safeguarded! This is why we call ourselves the “Best Friend of the Entrepreneur”.  
The scenario is scaled just as easily to the smallest companies as to the larger companies. The solution grows with your company. The standard solution can be customized using modules with the functionality you need!
