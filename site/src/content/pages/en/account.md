---
title: "Create an account"
path: "create-an-account"
description: "A Viscenario account lets tenants and guests register defects with the ViscenarioGO app at properties that have entered GPS coordinates for their buildings."
---

A Viscenario account can be used as a tenant/guest to register defects at the properties which have entered GPS coordinates for their buildings. This is typical of hotels and rental properties. Download ViscenarioGO to your smartphone and log in using this username and password.

If you have bought an apartment or are a sub-contractor, this will NOT WORK. The construction company will have to create your account and give you access to your apartment.

If you have bought an apartment the previous owner needs to give their permission to you.
